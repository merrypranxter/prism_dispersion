# Visual Targets

## 1. Dark Side
Sharp triangular prism, pure white beam entering from the left, vivid spectrum fan exiting to the right. Black background. The spectrum should be smooth, not banded, with all colors from deep violet to red clearly visible. The prism should be glassy, with slight reflections. The beam should be narrow, focused, intense. Iconic, minimalist, high contrast.

## 2. Diamond Fire
Brilliant-cut diamond, rotating slowly against a dark background. The diamond sparkles — bright white highlights from Fresnel reflection plus rainbow internal reflections from TIR + dispersion. The "fire" is the rainbow flashes that appear as the diamond rotates. High IOR (2.4) means lots of internal reflections. The diamond should feel precious, hard, brilliant. Dark background, high contrast, some bloom on bright highlights.

## 3. Liquid Glass
A transparent, wobbling blob (metaball SDF) in the center of the screen. A background image or gradient is refracted through it with rainbow fringes at the edges. The blob should feel like liquid glass — thick, viscous, slow-moving. The dispersion should be visible but subtle, not overwhelming. The blob should catch light and create caustic-like highlights on the background. The motion should be gentle, organic, hypnotic.

## 4. Spectral Caustics
Bright light source (small, intense) above a glass prism. The prism casts rainbow-fringed caustic pools on a white floor below. The pools should be sharp and colorful, not blurry. As the light source moves (or the prism rotates), the pools shift and shimmer. Each pool is a focal point where different wavelengths converge differently. The floor should be light-colored to show the caustics clearly. The overall effect should be like a disco ball made of physics — beautiful, precise, magical.
